# Frequently asked questions

## Is Wavebreak free and open source?

Free to download and use under its release EULA; proprietary and closed-source. This repository contains public documentation, community resources and approved binaries, not application source. Initial license/EULA text is marked for review before the first public software release.

## Where can I download it?

[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/rimedag/wavebreak/releases) is the canonical desktop channel. No first public binary release has been published yet.

## What should I expect from the alpha?

A usable experiment in focused browsing, with limitations. Site transformations and ad filtering can miss content or affect layouts. Some players require the original view. Browser migration and everyday compatibility are incomplete. Save notes manually and keep backups. Windows packages are unsigned; other platforms are not yet validated public downloads.

## Does ASCII convert every image or video?

No. Eligible images are converted locally or replaced with a labelled placeholder. Essential content and explicitly revealed images can remain original. The primary video remains available in ASCII; supported native players receive image-free controls in DEEP. Player detection is heuristic and cross-origin embeds may need to be revealed.

## Does blocking guarantee an ad-free web?

No. Blocking operates in all four modes, but sites and ad delivery change. A blocked-request count does not prove that every ad was removed.

## Is Private mode anonymous?

No. It separates temporary browsing from normal local session data; websites and network operators can still observe requests. Files you download remain on disk.

## Can I use an Obsidian vault?

You can edit ordinary Markdown files in a selected folder. Full Obsidian plugin, attachment, backlinks and rendering behavior is not implemented. See [data portability](data-portability.md).

## Does Stillpoint already work on iPad with Apple Pencil?

No. Standalone Stillpoint, iPad/iPhone apps and handwriting/drawing support are planned.

## Is Wavebreak an AI browser?

No. Its focus is user-controlled browsing, local notes and reduced distraction. It has no cloud AI feature or automatic note upload.
